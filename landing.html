Why sis.

Two moments every woman knows.

Group chats mean texting and waiting. Tracking apps share where you are all the time. sis. is a community of women you choose, and your location only when you decide.

If you go out with friends

"Text me when you get home."

We all say it, and then we wait, checking the phone. sis. turns it into one tap: your circle sees you're on your way, and knows when you've arrived.

If you've just moved, or you're travelling

"New city. I don't know anyone here."

Your people are far away. sis. helps you find circles of women and women nearby, so you're not on your own.

How it works

Three steps, one tap each.

  1. 1

    Join with an invite

    A woman already in sis. invites you. For now, you can also ask us for one.

  2. 2

    Create your circle

    Invite two friends with one link, or join a circle near you.

  3. 3

    Share when you decide

    On your way home, start Walk me home. Tap "I'm home" and sharing stops.

Our principle

Not tracking. Belonging.

  • Your location is off until you switch it on.
  • Only the circle you choose can see you.
  • Sharing stops by itself when you're home.
  • Leave and delete your account anytime.
  • We're adding identity verification, for example with Smart-ID.

We can't make the world safe.

But we can make sure you never walk through it alone.

sis. isn't an emergency service and can't replace 112. It's early, so some things will break. Tell us, and we'll fix them.

Questions

Things women ask us.

Is sis. free?

Yes. Your circle, sharing your location and SOS are free for every woman. If we add paid extras later, this core stays free.

Are sisters nearby verified?

Not yet. We're adding identity verification, for example with Smart-ID, before Sisters nearby opens to everyone.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open sis. in your phone's browser. On iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android tap ⋮, then Install app.

Where does sis. work?

Anywhere with a phone and the internet. Our first testers are in different countries; we launch first in Estonia.

Our story

Built by women who walk home too.

sis. started with a simple wish: that someone you trust knew where you were. Over 100 women in 11 countries told us they felt the same. We built the first version at Garage48 and won a Special Award, and now we're in the Garage48 accelerator.
