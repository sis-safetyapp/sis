Why sis.

Two moments we kept hearing about.

If you go out with friends

"Text me when you get home."

We all say it, and then we wait, checking the phone. sis. turns it into one tap: your circle sees you're on your way, and knows when you've arrived.

If you're new in the city, or travelling alone

"It's dark, and I don't know anyone here."

When your friends are far away, sis. shows verified women nearby and safe places with trained staff, so you're never really on your own.

How it works

From "text me" to "I'm home".

  1. 1

    Create your circle

    Invite the friends you trust with one link. Only they can see you, and only when you choose.

  2. 2

    Share your way home

    Start Walk me home or share a ride. Your circle follows you live, and sharing switches off when you arrive.

  3. 3

    Tap "I'm home"

    Your circle knows you're safe, no messages needed. If something feels wrong, hold SOS for two seconds instead.

Questions

Things women ask us.

Is sis. free?

Yes. The core is free for every woman: your circle, sharing your location and SOS. A Pro plan will add extras later.

Who can see my location?

Only the circle you choose, and only when you decide to share. Sharing switches off when you tap "I'm home".

How are sisters nearby verified?

Before launch, women who want to appear in Sisters nearby will verify their identity, for example with Smart-ID. Until then the beta is limited to people we invite.

Is sis. an emergency service?

No. sis. helps your circle look out for you, but it does not replace the emergency services. If you are in danger, call 112.

Do I need to download anything?

No. sis. works in your phone's browser and can be added to your home screen. Native apps for iPhone and Android are on our roadmap.

Where does sis. work?

We're starting in Estonia, with our first circles in Tallinn.
