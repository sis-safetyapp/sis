Why sis.

When your people are far away.

Sharing your location with your mum or a friend helps. But if something happens and they're in another city, they can't do much. In sis. there are also women near you, so you're not on your own.

If you go out with friends

"Text me when you get home."

We all say it, and then we wait for the message. In sis. your friends see that you're on your way, and when you're home.

If you've just moved, or you're travelling

"New city. I don't know anyone here."

Your friends are far away. In sis. you find women nearby, so you always have someone to ask.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. 1

    Get an invite

    A woman who's already in sis. invites you. No one to invite you? Write to us.

  2. 2

    Make your circle

    Add your friends, sisters, mum. Or join a circle of women near you.

  3. 3

    Share when you want

    Going home? Turn on Walk me home. When you get home, sis. tells your circle and sharing stops. You can also tap "I'm home" yourself.

Warn other women

Marks on the map

Saw a drunk group, someone following women, or a street with no lights? Put a mark on the map. Other women see it and can choose another way. Marks about people disappear after a few hours, marks about streets stay longer. If it's gone, women tap "Gone now" and the mark is removed.

Our principle

Not tracking. Belonging.

  • Your location is off until you turn it on.
  • Only the women you choose can see you.
  • When you're home, sharing stops and your location is deleted.
  • You can leave and delete your account anytime.
  • Coming soon: identity checks, for example with Smart-ID.

We can't make the world safe.

But we can make sure you're not alone in it.

sis. is not an emergency service. If you're in danger, call 112. We're still early and some things will break. Tell us and we'll fix them.

Questions

Things women ask us.

Is sis. only for women?

Yes. sis. is made by women, for women. You can join only with an invite from a woman who's already here. We're also working on a separate app for partners, male friends and relatives, so you can share your location with them when you need to.

Is everyone in sis. verified?

Not yet. You can only join with an invite, and women invite women they know. Soon we'll add identity verification with Smart-ID or Veriff. Until then, share your location only with women you know.

Do I need to download anything?

No. sis. opens in your phone's browser. To keep it on your home screen: on iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android tap ⋮, then Install app.

Where does sis. work?

Anywhere with a phone and the internet. Our first users are in different countries; we launch first in Estonia.

Our story

Built by women who need it too.

sis. started with a simple wish: that someone you trust knows you got home. More than 100 women in 11 countries told us they want the same. We built the first version at Garage48, won a Special Award, and now we're in the Garage48 accelerator.
