Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

sis. is a small, early-access community app for women, run by Jana and Swathi. This page explains in plain words what we keep about you, why, and how to remove it.

What we keep

The name you give us. Your phone number, only if you add it. Your Google account id and e-mail, which we receive when you sign in with Google. If you join a safety group, your location and SOS status while sharing is switched on. Places and reviews you add. Events you create or join, with your name and the list of who is going. Threads and replies you write. Reports you send about content, which only we can see.

Why

To sign you in, show your group where you are during a walk, and let you meet and talk with other women in the community. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we have no analytics or tracking tools.

Who sees it

Your name and phone are visible to the members of the groups you join. Your location is visible only to your group, and only while you share it. Places, reviews, events (including who is going), threads and replies you post are visible to other signed-in members. Reports are visible only to us, and the author is not told who reported. We can remove content that breaks our rules. We, the two founders, can access the database to run the service.

Where it is stored

With Supabase, our database provider, in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). Sign-in is handled by Google. Settings such as language are kept on your own device. The app is open to people from any country, and your data stays in the EU.

How long

Your location is overwritten each time it updates and is not kept as a history. Your location stops being shared and is erased when a group ends, and the group and its member list are deleted 7 days later. Everything else stays until you delete it or delete your account.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export and delete your data, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. You can edit your name and phone in your profile and leave a group at any time. To delete your account and all your data, use the “Delete my account and data” button in Settings; it works right away. If you cannot use it, write to us and we will do it within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Estonia this is the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon.

Legal basis

Your consent, which you give when you sign in and choose to share. You can take it back at any time.

If something goes wrong

If your data is ever exposed, we will tell the authority within 72 hours and tell you as soon as we can.

Not an emergency service

sis. does not replace calling emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call 112 or your local emergency number.
